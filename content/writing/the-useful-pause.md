There is a useful moment between reading something and deciding what it means. A little space lets the thought settle. Sometimes a page can make that pause feel possible.

## Room to notice

The small choices add up: an unhurried line, a heading you can find again, a margin that stays empty. They make room for the reader to notice what the writer has actually said.
